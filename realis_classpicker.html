Terms you'll see on class sheets ↓
Hallmark
A word or phrase in ❧these marks☙ inside a Sentence — a placeholder for something specific to your character. A weapon, a ship, a companion, a deity. You name it during character creation.
Orphan Vessel
A ship or vehicle without a registered owner — salvaged, stolen, or inherited. Several Sojourn classes have one as a Hallmark. Give it a name. No stats needed.
The Drift
The strange space between the moons. Not quite real. Some classes can navigate it; most treat it as a hazard. Ask the GM what it looks like in your campaign.
Ephemera
Objects your character carries that are useful but limited — vials, forged papers, calling cards. Unlike Sentences, Ephemera can be used up or lost. Your Class describes what your character tends to carry.
Moon Sentence
A Sentence that's true about the current location — established by the GM or a player spending a Token. You can invoke one as a Means in a Conflict.
Impulse
Your Sphere's recurring instinct. Act on it during a Conflict and your Token recharges — win or lose. It's printed in your Sphere's entry in the book.
Sympathetica
Objects tied to greater forces through a pact — specific to the Synecdochist class. Each one powers a Sentence. When a Sentence Realizes, one Sympathetica is crossed out.
