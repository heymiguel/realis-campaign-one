What is play by post?
We're not meeting at a table. We're playing in writing, on Discord, on our own schedules.

The GM posts a scene. Players write their characters' responses when they can — a few lines, a paragraph, whatever fits the moment. The story moves in pieces, over days and weeks.

There's no fixed session time. No one has to be online at the same time. You write when you can, the GM moves the story forward, and play builds up over time like a collaborative novel.

The main thing it asks of you: don't go silent. A few lines every couple of days keeps the game alive. If life gets in the way, just say so — the game waits for you.
