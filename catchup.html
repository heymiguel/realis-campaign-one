What to do now

Getting started.

1
Read the Player Guide
Especially the Sentences, Conflict, and class sections. You don't need to memorize it — just get a feel for how it works.
2
Use the Class Picker
Three questions, takes two minutes. Shows you classes that fit your instincts with their full Sentences.
3
Read the Charsheet Walkthrough
Watch Elodie build Maren step by step. Then fill out your own sheet in #realis-characters.
4
Write your Bonds toward existing characters
Read the character posts in #realis-characters. Write two Bonds toward two different people. Be specific.
5
Post in #realis-above-the-table
Introduce yourself, say who you're playing, and ask any questions. The group will bring you in from there.
